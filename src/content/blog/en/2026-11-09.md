## Demon's Souls, start to finish

On November 5, Soulbrandt, the new name of JonathanDC64’s DemonsSoulsRecomp, published its first release, 0.1.0, followed by 0.1.1 on the 7th ([entry](/en/recomp/demons-souls/), [GitHub](https://github.com/JonathanDC64/Soulbrandt)). It’s an alpha, but according to its README the game can already be beaten from start to finish: one of its testers finished it. It supports the US release (BLUS30443) and the European one (BLES00932), with its five languages, renders with DirectX 12 or Vulkan, and runs on Windows and Linux. It goes from 30 to 240 fps with the game’s timing fixed, and has online play on its own server, with messages, bloodstains, ghosts, co-op, and invasions, where players of both releases meet. Setup builds the game on your PC from your copy and decrypts its files with RPCS3. 58 of its last 100 commits list Claude as a co-author.

It isn’t the only one. Between the 4th and the 6th, timboat published three releases of another recompilation, independent of the first ([entry](/en/recomp/demons-souls-timboat/), [GitHub](https://github.com/timboat/demons-souls-recomp)). It also plays the whole game, with sound, music, and movies, though without online play yet. 60 fps is recommended, since sound drifts out of sync above it. On Linux and the Steam Deck it runs through Proton, and erikomis is working on a native build for Apple silicon Macs, on Metal, that so far reaches the start of the tutorial. 87 of its 97 commits list Claude as a co-author.

## Three Ninja Gaiden II recomps

TheSaltTrader’s [Ninja's Dawn](/en/recomp/ninja-gaiden-2/) now has company: two more recompilations of Ninja Gaiden II for Xbox 360, both on ReXGlue and both with their own Vulkan renderer instead of an emulated GPU.

- **openNG2**, by KamiTK2 ([entry](/en/recomp/ninja-gaiden-ii-kamitk2/), [GitHub](https://github.com/KamiTK2/openNG2)), published 1.0.5 on the 3rd and 1.1.0 on the 5th. Since that release it renders with Vulkan by default, which according to its author takes 64% less GPU time per frame than the Direct3D 12 path, still available. The logic runs at 60 fps, with an option that generates an in-between frame to reach 120. It has only been tested in the early chapters.
- **ng2recomp**, by gamboozino ([entry](/en/recomp/ninja-gaiden-ii-gamboozino/), [GitHub](https://github.com/gamboozino/ng2recomp)), published its first release on the 5th and reached 0.5.58 on the 8th, 14 releases in four days. It runs on Windows and Linux, and holds 60 fps on the Steam Deck: its author compares it with Xenia Edge, which averages 55.4, at 10 watts against 22. It needs Title Update 3; openNG2, Title Update 2. 42 of its last 100 commits list Claude as a co-author.

## A game that never shipped, from the Panasonic M2

On November 6, rsantos722 published releases 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 of a recompilation of IMSA presents World Championship Racing ([entry](/en/recomp/imsa-world-championship-racing/), [GitHub](https://github.com/rsantos722/imsa-wcr-m2-recomp)), a Studio 3DO racing game left unreleased when the Panasonic M2, the 3DO’s successor, was cancelled in 1997. It also includes shooter2d, the other game on the same disc. Its own recompiler translates the PowerPC 602 code to C, and its own program stands in for the console, with OpenGL 3.3 or a software renderer; MAME’s M2 driver served as a reference for the graphics chip. The README calls it fully playable and stable. It restores features the game left unfinished, all optional, and the launcher has a 3D car viewer. There are builds for Windows, Apple silicon macOS, and Linux, and it needs the prototype’s disc image, which the launcher checks.

## PS2 and PS3

- **[Twisted Metal: Black](/en/recomp/twisted-metal-black/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/TheBozos/TMB_Recomp)): 0.4.0, from the 6th, replaces the PS2Recomp fork of earlier releases with BozoEngine, The Bozos’ own recompiler. The executable no longer interprets any part of the PS2: meshes and textures are converted into native packs, and the VU programs become C++. Single matches, story mode, and 2 to 4 player split screen are playable; the endings are still to be checked. 85 of its last 100 commits list Codex as a co-author.
- **[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories](/en/recomp/grand-theft-auto-vice-city-stories-ps2/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/gothamor/gta-vcs-recomp)): gothamor published three launcher releases between the 4th and the 6th. It’s still in development: the game loop runs and the intro scene renders, with Direct3D 11 on Windows and Vulkan on Linux.
- **[Ratchet & Clank: Tools of Destruction](/en/recomp/ratchet-and-clank-future-tools-of-destruction/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/Re-TeRo17/ratchet-tod-recomp)): Re-TeRo17 published 1.1 on the 3rd and 1.2.0 on the 7th. It recompiles the European PS3 release with ps3recomp and Direct3D 12, and plays from the first planet, with every level loading, though the last ones have seen less play. It targets 60 fps, where the original runs at 30 in many places.
- **[Rogue Galaxy](/en/recomp/rogue-galaxy/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/victor-takai/rogue-galaxy-recomp)): 0.2.0, from the 4th, is a preview. The whole game can be played, but nobody has finished it with this release yet. It offers 60 fps, widescreen, and, as options, the Japanese voices and the changes from the Japanese Director’s Cut.

## Xbox and Xbox 360

- **[SoulCalibur II](/en/recomp/soulcalibur-ii-tmcleroy/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/tmcleroy/soulcalibur2-recomp)): tmcleroy published four betas between the 3rd and the 5th. It recompiles the Xbox release, with Spawn, and its launcher converts Link and Heihachi on your machine from your GameCube and PS2 discs. It runs on macOS, Linux, and Android, with a Windows build still untested, and has two-player online play with rollback.
- **[Top Spin 4](/en/recomp/top-spin-4/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/shaneomac1337/Top-Spin-4-Recomp)): 1.0.1, from the 7th, plays exhibition, career, and local multiplayer; online play doesn’t work.
- **[Anarchy Reigns](/en/recomp/anarchy-reigns/)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/VirtuaQuest/Anarchy-Reigns-Supreme)): on the 5th, VirtuaQuest opened the repo with the campaign already playable, for a game that was never rereleased. There’s no release: it’s built from source.

## Castlevania, from the Apple Arcade Mac build to Windows

On November 4, BLSNCVS published releases 1.0.0 through 1.0.7, all in one day, of a recompilation of Castlevania: Grimoire of Souls ([entry](/en/recomp/castlevania-grimoire-of-souls/), [GitHub](https://github.com/BLSNCVS/Castlevania-Grimoire-of-Souls-Recomp)). It takes the Apple Arcade macOS release: its code, C# compiled with IL2CPP for arm64, is translated to x64 and runs on Unity’s Windows player. The game’s server is emulated inside the program itself, so it works offline. It needs your .dmg of version 1.4.2. The README says AI was used to help with the code.
