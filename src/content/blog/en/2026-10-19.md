## God of War III Remastered, native on Windows

On October 10, at the very end of last week, Felipe Prado released the first version of a Windows port of God of War III Remastered for PS4 ([entry](/en/port/god-of-war-iii-remastered/), [GitHub](https://github.com/FelipePrado0/gow3_pc)). It comes from the same family as the Bloodborne port we covered last week: a fork of Supermedo’s Windows version, where the game’s original x86-64 code runs directly on the CPU and the graphics go to Vulkan through shadPS4’s video core.

This was the week it took off: on October 13 the Video Game Esoterica channel covered it ([video](https://youtu.be/M7bUqHo0Fkw)), and by October 14 it had 13 published versions, test builds included, and more than 120,000 downloads. According to its author, it’s playable from start to finish at native 1080p; at other resolutions some textures still look broken. You need your own PS4 dump of the game.

## Super Mario Galaxy: Nebula reaches 1.0

On October 13, keepvaibin released Nebula 1.0, a static recompilation of Super Mario Galaxy for Windows ([entry](/en/recomp/super-mario-galaxy/), [GitHub](https://github.com/keepvaibin/nebula)), a week after its first preview on October 6. The installer translates the game’s PowerPC code to C++ and compiles it on your PC from your own copy of the US version, so the download contains nothing from Nintendo.

The author still calls it a work in progress, and the README explains that the recompiler, the runtime, and the installer were written by AI agents under the author’s direction; the game’s logic, on the other hand, is the original code, translated instruction by instruction.

## Burnout 3: Takedown on Mac, Windows, Linux, and Steam Deck

Also on October 13, Takedawn 0.1.0 came out: a static recompilation of the Xbox version of Burnout 3: Takedown ([entry](/en/recomp/burnout-3-takedown/), [GitHub](https://github.com/xenophobentx/takedawn)). It builds on sp00nznet’s project and the xboxrecomp toolkit, and adds render scale up to 4x, a widescreen HUD fix, ultrawide, your own music, and 5.1 surround. The Android version will follow once a graphics bug found in the last tests is fixed. It joined the catalog through a pull request from its own author (#22).

## More news

- **[Shinobi III: Return of the Ninja Master](/en/port/shinobi-iii/)** (October 13): Ninja Master, a Rust reimplementation for Windows, Linux, and macOS that loads the graphics, music, and stages from your own cartridge. It’s experimental: all seven rounds are reachable, but a full playthrough isn’t guaranteed. With it, the Mega Drive / Genesis joins the catalog.
- **[Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time](/en/recomp/prince-of-persia-the-sands-of-time/)** (October 13): reSOT, a static recompilation of the Xbox builds, started the week with only the E3 2003 demo running. On October 16 its author pushed the NTSC retail version, which now reaches its first playable level, and a person has played the demo up to the third level. Both are still being tested, with crashes and sound pitched too high.
- **[Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition](/en/tool/xrevive/)** (October 13): with version 0.1.5 of xrevive, the launcher for Xbox games recompiled with Xrecomp, it becomes the first of them playable online, with an xb.live account. On October 14, 0.1.6 added a save editor and conversion of saves from the PS2 version.
- **[Ratchet & Clank](/en/port/ratchet-and-clank/)**: ReRAC, a Rust rewrite of the PS2 game, has been releasing alpha builds since October 5, and Video Game Esoterica covered it on October 14. Every level loads, but it isn’t playable from start to finish yet.
- **[Forza Horizon 2](/en/port/forza-horizon-2/)** (October 11, at the very end of last week): Azimuth runs the Xbox One version on Windows and Linux by translating the console’s calls to Win32 and DirectX 12. It’s the first Xbox One entry in the catalog; it’s published on itch.io, pay what you want and without source code.
- **Alley Cat and Saboteur II** (October 13 and 16): petarov released recompilations for Windows, Linux, and macOS of two DOS games, Alley Cat (1984) and Saboteur II: Avenging Angel (1987) ([Alley Cat](https://github.com/petarov/alleycat.exe), [Saboteur II](https://github.com/petarov/saboteur2)). They translate the original 8088 code to x86-64 assembly instead of emulating it, and add fullscreen and cheats; since October 17, Alley Cat can also be played [in the browser](https://petarov.github.io/alleycat.exe/). According to their READMEs, almost all of the code was generated by AI and only roughly reviewed. They’re not in the catalog because every download includes the original game.

Also new in the catalog: the recomps of [Mercenaries](/en/recomp/mercenaries/), [MediEvil](/en/recomp/medievil/), [Dead or Alive](/en/recomp/dead-or-alive/), [Banjo-Tooie](/en/recomp/banjo-tooie/), [TimeSplitters 2](/en/recomp/timesplitters-2/), and the first three Mega Drive Sonic games ([1](/en/recomp/sonic-the-hedgehog/), [2](/en/recomp/sonic-the-hedgehog-2/), and [3 & Knuckles](/en/recomp/sonic-3-and-knuckles/)), with their recompiler, [segagenesisrecomp](/en/tool/segagenesisrecomp/); the ports of [Crash Team Racing: Turbocharged](/en/port/crash-team-racing-turbocharged/), [Star Fox Adventures](/en/port/star-fox-adventures/), and [sm64coopdx](/en/port/sm64coopdx/); and the [OutRun2006Tweaks](/en/mod/outrun-2006-tweaks/) mod. The [Namco System 21 / 22 / 23](/en/decomp/namco-system-22/) decompilation now covers ten arcade games, including both Time Crisis titles.
